Privacy, Anonymity & Safety Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 • HKHC Church Community Platform

Core Commitment: Our goal is to provide a psychologically safer way for youths, teens, and adults to ask difficult, sensitive, or embarrassing questions without fear of social judgment, while maintaining a safe, accountable community environment.

1. How Anonymity Works: Two Distinct Modes

In our platform, anonymity is a per-post choice made each time you submit a question or reply. It is not an account-wide setting. For every post, you are explicitly asked:

1. Post with my identity: Your chosen community display name and profile picture will appear alongside your post.
2. Post anonymously: Your post will be presented to other ordinary community members with the label "Anonymous Member".

When you post anonymously:

  • Other community members cannot see your real name or display name.
  • Other members cannot see your email address or user account profile.
  • Our application strictly obscures your internal user ID from HTML markup, JavaScript objects, URLs, and API responses.

2. Anonymity is Relative, Not Absolute

Because our community includes teenagers and vulnerable individuals, moderation is a first-class safety feature. To prevent bullying, self-harm, predatory behavior, harassment, and severe misconduct:

  • Every post is internally associated with your authenticated church account in our secure database.
  • Authorized pastoral moderators and administrators have the ability to inspect the internal author of an anonymous post solely when investigating safety concerns, reports of abuse, threats, or severe policy violations.
  • Moderators cannot casually expose your identity to other members. All moderator investigative views and actions are permanently recorded in a secure, immutable audit log.

3. Authentication vs. Public Presentation

We maintain a strict architectural boundary between your:

  • Authentication Identity: Your private email address and password hash used for login. This is NEVER published or visible to other members.
  • Public Display Identity: Your public community display name and optional bio/avatar, displayed when you choose identified posting.
  • Anonymous Presentation: A neutral, privacy-protected presentation showing only "Anonymous Member" to ordinary users.

4. Reporting and Audit Accountability

Any community member can report posts that violate community guidelines. Reports are queued for pastoral review. Every action taken by a moderator—such as hiding content, issuing warnings, or resolving reports—is logged with a timestamp and stated reason to ensure pastoral accountability.

5. Questions and Pastoral Inquiries

If you have questions about how your privacy is handled or need pastoral support regarding a sensitive situation, please reach out to our pastoral team directly or through church leadership.
